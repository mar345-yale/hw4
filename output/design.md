# Homework 4 — Design Changes (Problem 10)

Front-end-only styling to make the site feel more welcoming. No functionality changed.

| Change | Why it helps customers stick around and buy |
|---|---|
| **White-and-blue theme** (replaced the dark black/blue look) | A bright, clean storefront feels friendlier and more trustworthy, so shoppers stay and browse instead of bouncing. |
| **Handsome Dan mascot** on the homepage (hand-drawn SVG bulldog in his Y bandana) | A warm, on-brand welcome builds an emotional Yale connection and invites shoppers to engage with the chat and the catalogue. |
| **Dog bark on chat replies** (with a mute toggle) | A playful, memorable touch reinforces the brand personality and makes chatting feel alive, nudging shoppers to keep asking and discovering products. |
| **Bigger "Campus Customs" heading** | A bold, confident hero makes the brand instantly clear and signals a real store, building the confidence shoppers need to buy. |
